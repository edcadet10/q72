# Governance

Q-72 uses maintainer-led, evidence-seeking governance while the project is small.

## Roles

- **Contributors** report playtests, propose scenarios, improve documentation, or submit code.
- **Reviewers** are repeat contributors trusted to review in an area they understand.
- **Maintainers** merge changes, manage releases and community spaces, and protect the project's validation rules.

The repository owner is the initial maintainer. Maintainer or reviewer status is earned through a pattern of constructive contributions and sound review, not through job title or employer.

## Decisions

Small, reversible choices are resolved in pull requests. Changes to project scope, scoring philosophy, evidence thresholds, privacy, or governance begin as public design issues and remain open for at least seven days when practical. Maintainers make the final call and document the reasoning, dissent, and evidence considered.

No employer or sponsor receives privileged control over scenarios, results, or roadmap decisions. Real company and customer data are out of scope.

## Releases

The `main` branch is the public playable build. Merges require passing automated checks and maintainer review. Releases should summarize gameplay changes and any effect on prior validation evidence.
