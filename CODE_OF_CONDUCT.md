# Community code of conduct

Q-72 should be a place where operations practitioners, game designers, researchers, and engineers can challenge ideas without demeaning people.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, curious, and respectful.
- Critique decisions and evidence, not a person's competence or identity.
- Welcome differing levels of technical and domain experience.
- Protect participant privacy and never solicit confidential company or customer data.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect a project decision.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable in any project space.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, temporarily limit participation, or ban participants when necessary to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and documented without exposing private information.

For conduct visible on GitHub, use the report option on the relevant comment and notify the repository owner through a contact method on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/edcadet10). If the repository owner is involved, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.
